Motivating Application Typology:  
---

1) **Open Digital Infrastructure \-** Technologies and protocols that are used as foundational tools within digital services that anyone with technical sophistication could create, configure, or deploy.  
- (**CNP**) Core networking protocols  
- (**AUT**) Authentication mechanisms  
- (**1-BLK**) Blockchains  
- (**ML**) Machine learning  
- (**NWT**) Network telemetry  
- (**DAD**) Data aggregation   
2) **Proprietary digital infrastructure \-** Technologies and protocols that are used as foundational tools within digital services but are highly centralized or require significant capital to create, configure, or deploy.  
- (CC) Cloud computation  
- (DDC) Databases and Data Centers  
- (HWM) Hardware manufacturers  
3) **Governance, civic work, and physical infrastructure \-** Technologies that governments directly manage, citizens interact with as part of their civic engagement, or are embedded directly into our physical infrastructure.  
- (DPI) Digitized physical infrastructure  
- (SPG) Statistics used in public governance  
- (PH) Public health, including COVID-19  
- (NIST) National Institute of Standards and Technology  
- (VOT) Voters and voting systems  
- (GVE) Governance, governments, and government employees  
- (LE) Law enforcement  
- (NGO) Non-governmental organizations  
4) **Products and features provided by organizations (both for-profit and non-profit) \-** Technologies that serve to improve the product, service, or other kind of commercial object offered by a company to end-users.   
- (ADD) Digital advertising  
- (WEB) Web services  
- (MSG) Messaging services  
- (LOC) Location services  
- (BNK) Banking  
- (PWD) Password managers  
- (DLD) Data leak detection  
- (RS) Ride sharing  
- (SE) Software Engineering  
5) **Specifically named groups of people \-** Explicit references to peoples.  
- (JUR) Journalists  
- (ACT) Activists  
- (MIN) Minorities, people experiencing marginalization, or those lacking access to resources  
- (PGTS) People experiencing government surveillance or censorship  
- (IPS) People experiencing interpersonal surveillance or abuse  
6) **Finance \-** Technological tools that are used to facilitate the flow of currencies, assets, and values  
- (6-BLK) Blockchains  
- (BNK) Banking  
- (CUR) Currenties, e-cash, post-quantum cash  
- (ASC) Auctions and smart contracts  
7) **Non-categorized motivating applications**  
- (CRY) Cryptographers and related academics  
- (PQC) Post-quantum developments  
- (BMD) Bioinformatics and medical data
