Cryptography, Cryptanalysis, and Out of Scope Definitions:  
---

(1) **Cryptography paper** \-  a paper that is *centered* around constructing or designing a novel cryptographic object or cryptosystem, with analysis of extant cryptosystems as related or prior work. Look for: 1\) a definition of security, 2\) a proof that a construction meets the given security definition.  
	*Note: Papers with formal analysis as proofs should be marked as confused and brought to the entire team.*

(2) **Cryptanalysis paper** \- a paper that is *centered* around analyzing an existing cryptographic object or cryptosystem—possibly identifying flaws—with ideas or designs for improving cryptographic objects or cryptosystems as potential secondary contributions. Look for: 1\) identification of a particular cryptographic object or cryptosystem and 2\) a method of analysis— which can be theoretical (referring to mathematical definitions and proofs) and/or practical (referring to software and hardware implementation)—that demonstrates potential or lived vulnerabilities in that cryptographic object or cryptosystem.  
	*Note: Papers that give new proofs for already deployed protocols are cryptanalysis papers.*

(3) **Out of scope papers \-** Papers that do provide some sort of security analysis but said analysis is not a proof that is not a formal mathematical analysis of the security model. Not cryptography/cryptanalysis paper.
